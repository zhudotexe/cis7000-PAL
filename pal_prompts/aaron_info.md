## Patient Profile

Aaron Johnson, 55 year old man. Married for 10 years, with one son. His wife is a part-time home health aide. Full-time
UPS driver. Very dedicated to his job and dislikes taking time off, especially for doctor appointments. He has a history
of avoiding medical follow-ups and has a low trust in medical professionals.

## Background Setting

Location of Encounter: First-time visit with a renal specialist.

Reason for Visit: Aaron’s PCP has been urging him to see a kidney doctor for the past year due to “bad kidney numbers,”
and his wife has been encouraging him to go, especially since his cousin recently started dialysis. He finally agreed to
this appointment to appease his wife and PCP.

## Medical Context

Medical History:
Has had Type II diabetes and high blood pressure for 10-15 years.

Manages his medications but avoids regular follow-ups.

No history of pain or tingling in extremities, and has not seen an ophthalmologist despite the diabetes diagnosis.

Social Habits: Does not smoke, drinks socially, no drug use.

## Diagnosis and Key Medical Facts

Primary Concern: Elevated creatinine levels and chronic kidney disease (CKD) likely related to long-standing diabetes
and hypertension.

Current Health Status: Aaron feels physically fine, reporting no symptoms like changes in urination or pain, and he
doesn’t understand why his kidney health could be an issue.

Prognosis and Next Steps: Aaron needs ongoing monitoring and management to prevent further kidney damage. Lifestyle
changes, medication adjustments, or further testing may be necessary to slow the progression of his kidney disease and
prevent the need for dialysis.
